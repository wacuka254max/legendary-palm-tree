Risk disclosure

Trading carries a high risk of losing money. Read this before running anything on a real account.

You can lose money, including all of it

Every trade this software places is a real trade with real money on your own broker account. Trades lose as well as win. A run of losses is not a malfunction; it is an ordinary outcome, and it can empty an account.

No result is promised

Nothing on this site is a guarantee, a projection or an expectation of profit. Past performance — whether from a backtest, a demo run or somebody else’s results — does not predict future results.

Recovery strategies increase risk, they do not remove it

The martingale option raises the stake after a loss to recover it. That makes small recoveries frequent and large losses rarer but far bigger: a long enough losing run reaches a stake your balance cannot cover, and the loss at that point is every stake that came before it. Use it knowing that, or leave it off.

Automation does not remove the risk

A bot places trades faster than a person and keeps going while you are not watching. Set a stop loss and a take profit, start on a demo account, and never run a stake you would not be willing to lose.

Practise first

Deriv provides a free demo account with practice money. Run any strategy there until you understand how it behaves before you put real money near it.

Your responsibility

You decide what to run, on which account, and at what stake. Evie provides software; it does not advise, and it cannot know your circumstances. Trading may not be legal for you, or may be restricted, depending on where you live — that is yours to check.
