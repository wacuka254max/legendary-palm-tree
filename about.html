About Evie

Evie is free software for automating trades on a trading account you already own. It is not a broker, not a fund, and not an investment scheme.

What Evie is

A web app that reads a market and places trades on it. You connect your own Deriv account through Deriv’s official OAuth sign-in, choose the market and the trade type, and the bot places those trades on your account under the limits you set. You can stop it at any time, and it stops itself when it reaches a target or a loss limit you chose.

It is free. There is no subscription, no licence fee, no deposit to us, and nothing to buy.

What Evie is not

  • Not a broker or an exchange. Evie holds no client money and has no accounts. Your money stays with your own regulated broker, and every trade is executed by that broker on your own account.
  • Not a deposit-taking business. Evie never asks you to send funds anywhere, and never will. Anyone asking you to deposit money with “Evie” is not us.
  • Not a managed account or a signal service. Nobody trades on your behalf. The software runs in your own browser, on your own instruction.
  • Not financial advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to trade or to buy any instrument.
  • Not a guarantee of profit. No automated strategy can promise a return, and this one does not. Losing trades are a normal part of how it works — see the risk disclosure.

How your account is connected

Sign-in goes through Deriv’s own OAuth page, on Deriv’s domain. Evie never sees your Deriv password. The token that comes back is stored in your own browser and is used only to read your balance and place the trades you ask for. Disconnecting from the dashboard removes it.

Who runs it

Evie is an independent software project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Deriv, MetaQuotes, TradingView, Binance, Exness, OANDA, or any prop firm named on this site; those names and logos appear only to say which platforms the software works with.
