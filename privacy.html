Privacy

The short version: there is no account to create here, and what the app stores stays in your own browser.

What we do not collect

No sign-up, no name, no email address, no password, no identity document, no card and no bank details. There is nothing to upload and nothing to pay, so none of it is asked for.

Your Deriv sign-in

Signing in happens on Deriv’s own domain, through their OAuth page. Your Deriv password is never seen by this site. The access token Deriv returns is kept in your browser’s storage on your own device and is sent only to Deriv, to read your balance and place the trades you ask for. It never reaches a server of ours — there is no server of ours holding it. Disconnecting deletes it.

What is stored on your device

  • The Deriv token, until you disconnect.
  • Your light or dark preference, and which account you last used.
  • Settings for the current tab — stake, limits and similar — which are cleared when the tab closes.
  • A short cache of recent prices, so a page reopens on data rather than a placeholder.

Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking scripts on this site.
